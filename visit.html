Who you’ll meet

“They are true quilters and love sharing what they love with others!” is how one Knoxville customer put it. The class pages name a few of the people who teach: Dana, Darlene, Toni, Jenny and Pat.

Before you drive over

  • Bringing a machine in? Fill out a drop-off tag first.
  • Signing up for a class? Reservations are necessary, by phone or email, for each class.
  • Looking for one particular thing? Call. If it’s not in stock, they will try to order it for you.
